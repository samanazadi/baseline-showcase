Scope Root Only

Outside the divs: not in scope

Between the divs: in scope

Inside the inner div: still in scope

Inline Scope

Outside the divs: not in scope

Between the divs: in scope

Inside the inner div: still in scope

Donut Scope

Outside the divs: not in scope

Between the divs: in scope

Scoping Proximity

Outside the divs: not in scope

Between the divs: only the outer scope applies

Inside the inner div: both scopes match, the closer root wins
